# Vision

## Core Philosophy

**Strategy before style.** BrandForge AI is not a logo generator. It's an AI Branding Consultant that understands the essence of a business before creating any visual identity.

The AI thinks like a strategist first — analyzing positioning, archetypes, emotional drivers, and competitive dynamics — before it ever suggests a color or shape. Every output must feel bespoke and original, never templated or generic.

## Our Principles

1. **Strategy-First** — Visual identity emerges from strategy, not the other way around.
2. **Radically Original** — No "coffee shop = coffee cup" logic. Every output is bespoke.
3. **Agency Quality, AI Speed** — $5,000 agency depth delivered in minutes.
4. **Built for Founders** — For people who refuse to settle for a generic logo and a Canva template.

## Target Customer

Founders, indie makers, and small business owners who:
- Care deeply about their brand
- Need professional brand strategy but can't afford a $5K agency engagement
- Want original, thoughtful output — not templated slop
- Value depth over speed, quality over quantity
